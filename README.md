# Disney Promotion for Eligible Customers

## Product Overview

The Disney Promotion for Eligible Customers initiative will identify customers who meet defined eligibility criteria and provide them with a targeted Disney promotional offer.

## Business Problem

Customers who qualify for promotional offers need to be identified accurately and consistently. The solution should ensure that eligible customers receive the appropriate promotion while ineligible customers are excluded.

## Product Goal

Create a reliable end-to-end process that:

1. Identifies eligible customers
2. Determines the appropriate promotion
3. Generates and delivers the promotion
4. Allows eligible customers to redeem the promotion
5. Tracks promotion performance and redemption

## Target Customers

Customers who meet the defined business eligibility criteria for the Disney promotion.

## Key Product Capabilities

- Customer eligibility determination
- Promotion eligibility rules
- Promotion generation
- Customer notification
- Promotion redemption
- Redemption tracking
- Product reporting and analytics

## Success Metrics

- Eligibility accuracy
- Promotion delivery rate
- Promotion redemption rate
- Customer conversion rate
- Number of duplicate or invalid promotions
- Customer experience

## MVP Scope

The initial release will focus on:

- Customer eligibility
- Promotion generation
- Customer notification
- Promotion redemption
- Basic reporting

## Out of Scope

Advanced personalization, predictive analytics, and additional promotional programs are outside the initial MVP scope.
